The project

About SEErch²

An independent search engine project focused on making web discovery more flexible, customizable and accessible.

Our idea

SEErch² is an experimental search project. Its goal is to provide another way to explore the web while giving users more control over their search experience.

What we're working on

  • Web, image and video discovery.
  • Flexible search result ranking.
  • Safe Search controls across result types.
  • A simple, lightweight and responsive interface.
  • Continued improvements to search quality and usability.

Independent by design

SEErch² is an evolving project. Features and services may change as development continues.
